Supplementary Table 4 | Monte Carlo propagation of uncertainty in the surge annual loss. Supplementary Table 5 | Decomposition of the NFIP shortfall and counterfactuals (Fig. 4; Supplementary Fig. 3). Loss on the NSI × USACE × NACCS basis; 36961 contracts. Upper bound = min(block-group loss, block-group cover). Expected = take-up × min(structure loss, mean cover per contract). Gap: no policy = (1 − take-up) × loss; gap: cover < loss = take-up × max(loss − cover, 0). Counterfactuals: every structure covered at its block group's mean cover per contract, or at the statutory limit ($250,000 per residential unit, $500,000 non-residential). LMI groups are tract quartiles of the HUD/CDBG low-to-moderate-income population share; take-up is contracts per NSI structure inside the 1-in-100-yr footprint.

Supplementary Table 6 | NFIP cover against rainfall building loss, surge-side versus rain-side block groups (data for Fig. 3b). Loss is computed on the DEP stormwater footprints (moderate ≈1-in-18 yr, extreme ≈1-in-458 yr) with the NSI × USACE loss chain. A block group is surge-side if at least half of its structure value lies inside the 1-in-1,000-yr still-water reach and rain-side otherwise; block groups with less than half of their structure value inside the surge model domain are reported separately. Upper bound = min(block-group loss, block-group building cover); expected = take-up × min(structure loss, mean cover per contract) with policies placed at random in the block group. The extreme scenario is tied to 2080 sea level in the DEP model.
